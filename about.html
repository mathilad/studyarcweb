ABOUT US

A clearer path through your study journey.

Study Arc is an ArcDex app created by Mathila Dinimuthu to help students turn their study goals into consistent, focused work.

Built with Sri Lankan A/L students in mind, it brings planning, study sessions and progress into one place, so your next step is easier to see.

Download the app · v1.0.5 How Study Arc works
OUR PURPOSE

Make steady progress feel manageable.

Classes, revision, papers and everyday life compete for your time. Study Arc helps you organize that time and give attention to the areas that need more practice.

Plan around real life

A useful study plan should fit your available hours, classes and rest. Study Arc connects your schedule with your learning priorities.

Focus on the session

A simple stopwatch and laps help you track the work you actually do, with sessions organized by subject and topic.

Learn from your progress

Study history, revision needs and weak topics help you reflect on your preparation and decide what to work on next.

Progress you can build on

Our aim is to support better habits and clearer preparation. Your progress is a guide for your next steps, not a promise of an exam grade.
