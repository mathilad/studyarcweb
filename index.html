EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE

Built around the way you actually study.

Study Arc is more than a timetable. Your plan changes based on what you study, where you struggle, and how close you are to important tests and exams.

⌁

Adaptive Timetable

Plans study sessions around your classes, sleep schedule, available time and current priorities.

◴

Focused Study Timer

Track real study time with a clean stopwatch, laps and subject-based session history.

↗

Weak Topic Detection

Test results and practice performance push weaker areas higher in your future study plan.

◎

Progress Tracking

See studied dates, completed sessions, time spent and consistency without unnecessary clutter.

↻

Smart Revision

Previously studied topics return at useful intervals so revision becomes part of the plan.

◇

Exam Preparation

As exams approach, Study Arc gradually shifts the balance toward revision and past-paper work.
